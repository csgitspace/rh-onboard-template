  1. Set up Google 2-Step Verification / Multi-Factor Authentication.
  2. Bookmark wnyric.eschooldata.com and make sure you can successfully sign in to eSchoolData.
  3. Sign into Google Chrome with your Roy-Hart account and turn on Sync.
  4. Make Google Chrome your default browser.
  5. Install and sign into Google Drive for Desktop and begin using your Google Drive as your primary location for storing files.
Security Requirement
Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication

Please make setting up 2-Step Verification one of the very first things you do with your new Roy-Hart Google account.

Erie 1 BOCES security requirements require Multi-Factor Authentication on district Google accounts. New accounts have a two-week window to complete this setup.

If you do not configure 2-Step Verification during that window, your account may be locked until the security requirement is addressed.

To get started:

  1. Sign into your Roy-Hart Google account.
  2. Open your Google Account settings.
  3. Choose Security & sign-in.
  4. Under How you sign in to Google, select 2-Step Verification.
  5. Follow Google's prompts to configure your second verification method.

Your second step may use a Google prompt on your phone, an authenticator, passkey, security key, or another available verification method. We strongly recommend configuring a backup method as well.

Always Use Google Chrome

Google Chrome is the browser you should use for district-related Internet access.

District-managed bookmarks, extensions, settings, and many security policies are deployed through Chrome. Microsoft Edge may be installed on your computer, but it should not be your primary browser for district work.

On Windows 11, go to Settings → Apps → Default Apps → Google Chrome → Set Default.

Next, sign into the Chrome browser itself using your Roy-Hart Google account and turn on Sync. This is important because it allows district-managed resources and policies to be applied to your browser.

Roy-Hart Resources & Staff Bookmarks

Once Chrome Sync is enabled, right-click your bookmarks bar and make sure "Show Roy-Hart Resources" is checked.

This gives you access to our district-managed Staff Bookmarks, which contain many of the applications and resources you'll use regularly.

You can find things such as building phone extensions, documents shared by your building principal, QWare work orders, district applications, and other commonly used resources here.

Use Google Drive for Your Files

Please use your Google Drive as your primary location for saving district files rather than storing important documents only on the local computer.

You can self-install Google Drive for Desktop. Once installed and signed in, Google Drive appears as the G: drive on your Windows computer.

This allows you to open and save files directly from applications such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and others without continually uploading and downloading files.

Drive for Desktop can also be installed on your classroom Cleartouch interactive panel.

Please Read
Student Data Privacy

New websites and educational apps can be incredibly useful, but we cannot simply upload student information into any website or service we find online.

New York State Education Law 2-D and district policy require us to protect personally identifiable student information.

A few important rules:

  • Do not allow students to use "Log in with Google" on a website unless that service has been approved by the district.
  • Do not enter student names, email addresses, IDs, grades, phone numbers, addresses, testing information, or other personally identifiable information into an unapproved application.
  • Never provide grades, behavior records, IEP information, 504 information, medical information, or other educational record data to an unapproved service.
  • Before introducing a new app or website that collects student information, check whether it is already approved.
